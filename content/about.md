## What this site is

Emvela is an independent editorial site covering personal wellness
products and the everyday habits around them: materials and safety, buying
online, and general wellbeing. It is written for adults aged 18 and over.

We are not a shop. We do not sell products, and we do not accept payment in
exchange for a favourable review. If that ever changes, it will be stated on the
page it applies to, in plain language, above the fold.

## How we source what we publish

Every guide is put together from primary material wherever possible:

- **Manufacturer documentation** — material data sheets, safety documentation,
  care instructions and warranty terms.
- **Published standards** — for example the material-safety and labelling
  frameworks that consumer goods are assessed against in the EU, the UK and
  North America.
- **Peer-reviewed literature** — mainly for the human side of the subject:
  stress, sleep, body image and relationship communication.
- **Regulator and consumer-body guidance** — recall notices, certification
  registries and published consumer advice.

Where a claim rests on a single small study, we say so. Where the evidence is
mixed, we say that too, rather than resolving it into a confident sentence the
literature does not support. Where we simply do not know, we write that we do
not know.

## What we do not do

- We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you have a health
  concern, speak to a qualified healthcare professional.
- We do not publish explicit imagery. This is a text-led reference site.
- We do not publish content aimed at anyone under 18.

## Corrections

If something here is wrong, we want to know. Write to
[hello@emvela.com](mailto:hello@emvela.com)
with the page address and what you believe is incorrect. Substantive
corrections are made to the article itself; where the change affects the
conclusion, we note that the article was updated and when.

## Editorial independence

Our editorial team decides what gets published. Commercial relationships, if we
ever enter any, do not carry the right to review or approve editorial copy
before publication, and do not carry the right to have critical material removed
afterwards.
