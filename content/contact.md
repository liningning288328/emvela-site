## Get in touch

We read everything that arrives, although we cannot always reply individually.
For a correction, please include the page address and, if you have one, a source
we can check.

- **Editorial, corrections and general enquiries:**
  [hello@emvela.com](mailto:hello@emvela.com)

## Response times

We aim to acknowledge editorial correspondence within five working days.
Corrections to published articles are prioritised.

## What we cannot help with

We are an editorial site rather than a retailer or a support desk, so we are not
able to help with order status, delivery, returns, warranty claims or product
faults. Those need to go to whichever retailer you bought from — they hold the
order record and are the ones who can act on it.

We also cannot give medical advice or interpret symptoms. Please speak to a
qualified healthcare professional.

## Before you write

It may save you time to check the [About](/about/) page first, which sets out how
we source material and how we handle corrections. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/)
explains what happens to any personal information you send us.
