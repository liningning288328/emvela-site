Most of what is written about personal wellness products online is either a
sales page or a listicle that has never been near a lab. We started this site
because the useful questions — what is this made of, will it still be safe in a
year, will the box say what is inside — are the ones nobody answers plainly.

So that is what we do. No affiliate-driven rankings, no invented clinical
claims, no pretending that a topic is complicated when it is not.
